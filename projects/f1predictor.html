What it does

A machine-learning pipeline that predicts Formula 1 podium probabilities (P1, P2, P3) for upcoming races. It is trained on 2014–2024 data. Inputs are qualifying results, driver and team history, circuit characteristics and live weather. An XGBoost and PyTorch ensemble produces calibrated probabilities, shown in a Streamlit dashboard.

Prediction pipeline

01
Data ingestion. Qualifying results (OpenF1), race results 2014–2024 (FastF1), circuit data and live weather (Open-Meteo).
02
Features. Driver form, team performance trends, grid position and weather-adjusted pace.
03
Ensemble. Three XGBoost binary classifiers, one per podium position, plus a PyTorch MLP with driver and team embeddings. A logistic regression stacks their outputs.
04
Calibration. Isotonic regression makes the probabilities reliable for every podium position.
05
Dashboard. A Streamlit app shows the probabilities race by race.

Model architecture

XGBoost layer
Three binary classifiers (P1, P2, P3), each trained separately on the engineered features
PyTorch layer
MLP with learned driver and constructor embeddings, which capture team and driver identity
Stacking
Logistic regression combines the XGBoost and PyTorch outputs
Calibration
Isotonic regression calibrates the final probabilities

Data sources

  • FastF1Historical race results, lap times and telemetry (2014–2024 seasons)
  • OpenF1Live qualifying data for upcoming races
  • Open-MeteoCircuit weather: temperature, rain probability, wind speed

Evaluation

Leave-one-season-out cross-validation, plus a 2023–2024 holdout set that was not used in training. Metrics: Brier score, log loss, ROC-AUC, winner accuracy and podium overlap.
